Clan Maze

Four dens sit at the corners of a square maze. Clanmates leave the den to patrol. Play as deputy and a few warriors may follow you; play as medicine cat and injured clanmates come to you. Deputies lead battle patrols more often than hunting patrols. Prey brought home usually raises a warrior, with a 10% chance of a deputy and a 5% chance of a medicine cat.

  • ThunderClan — 40% extra warrior when a deputy or leader brings prey home.
  • ShadowClan — at night, 50% chance to deal 3 damage instead of 1.
  • WindClan — 1.5× faster than the other clans.
  • RiverClan — 20% chance to take no damage from an enemy warrior.
  • Dogs — 20 HP strays appear at the maze center every minute, and several can hunt at once. At night they go back to the middle and sleep.
